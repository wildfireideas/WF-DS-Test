Decisions needed from the client

  1. Online ordering or not. v2 uses a Quote List as the default. If Shopify B2B is chosen, Quote List becomes cart, checkout and order history, and My Account grows to match.
  2. Connection to the inventory and pricing system. Public stock and gated pricing both depend on it, whichever platform wins.
  3. Confirm: the efficiency minimums (#7), the credit application's guarantor and consent wording (#19), the real branch list, HQ address, inventory count and any ownership messaging.

Critical 7

#1

Account terms were mixed up

Problem in v1
“Register,” “Open an Account,” “Create Account” and “Apply for Credit” blurred two different things: online access for existing customers and a credit account for new ones.
What v2 does
One “Become a Customer” page asks how you buy today, then branches to the online-access form or the credit application. The same labels are used everywhere.
Fixed in v2See it in v2 (opens in a new tab)
#2

Ordering buttons in a sitemap without ordering

Problem in v1
“Add to Order” and an “Account” tab existed, but there was no cart, checkout or account page.
What v2 does
“Add to Order” became “Add to Quote List,” and a My Account page was added (quote lists, invoices, company users). If Shopify B2B is approved, this becomes cart, checkout and order history.
Needs client decisionSee it in v2 (opens in a new tab)
#3

No search results or product pages

Problem in v1
Parts search is a top task, but search had no results page and product titles linked nowhere.
What v2 does
New Search Results template (exact part and cross-reference matches, no-results help) and Product Detail template (specs, documents, stock at nearby branches).
Fixed in v2See it in v2 (opens in a new tab)
#4

Guests couldn't see stock

Problem in v1
Locking availability behind login works against the brief's “verify stock” need.
What v2 does
Availability is public (In stock / Limited / Out at my branch, plus the 3 nearest on product pages). Only price and quantity need a login.
Fixed in v2See it in v2 (opens in a new tab)
#5

Menus failed on iPad and for screen readers

Problem in v1
Hover/focus-only dropdowns don't open on iOS Safari taps and didn't report open or closed.
What v2 does
Menus open on click or tap, report their state to screen readers, and close with Esc or an outside click.
Fixed in v2See it in v2 (opens in a new tab)
#6

ZIP search found nothing

Problem in v1
Every ZIP code returned “No branches match.”
What v2 does
Search by ZIP, city, state or location. Results sort by distance with miles shown, and out-of-area ZIPs get a clear message. The prototype approximates ZIP locations; production needs a geocoding API.
Fixed in v2See it in v2 (opens in a new tab)
#7

An efficiency option that's illegal to install

Problem in v1
“13.4 SEER2” is below the 2023 regional minimum in all seven states.
What v2 does
System Builder and filters start at 14.3 SEER2, with a short note explaining the rule.
Fixed · client to confirmSee it in v2 (opens in a new tab)
